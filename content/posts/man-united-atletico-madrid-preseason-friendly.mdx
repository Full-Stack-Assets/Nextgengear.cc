## What happened
The match kicked off under clear skies, with United dominating early possession and looking to build from the back. At the 5‑minute mark, a free‑kick on the left side was taken quickly, and Ortiz’s corner whipped into the far post, slipping past Heaton for the opening goal.

United responded with several chances, including a volley from Shaw that Oblak tipped over the bar and a header from Yoro that went wide. Despite sustained pressure, the Red Devils could not breach the Spanish defence, and the first half ended 1‑0 to Atletico.

In the second half, United continued to press, with Mount delivering a superb cross to Shaw and Amad testing the keeper, but Atletico remained compact, preserving their lead until the final whistle.
