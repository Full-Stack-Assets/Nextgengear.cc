The Wyndham Championship at Sedgefield Country Club is the final regular‑season stop before the FedExCup Playoffs, and PGA TOUR’s expert panel has released their fantasy and betting recommendations. Their analysis highlights a mix of seasoned tour veterans and rising prospects poised to make a splash. For fantasy managers and prop bettors, these picks could be the difference between climbing the leaderboard or falling short.

<Callout type="takeaway">Underdogs Blades Brown and Ryan Gerard provide the strongest value for fantasy rosters and prop bets at Wyndham.</Callout>

## What happened
Will Gray, senior manager for TOUR & Golfbet editorial, backs Blades Brown (+265) after his recent Korn Ferry Tour form and a near‑playoff miss, and he also highlights Nico Echavarria (+188) as a South American contender who has finished T22 or better the last two years at Sedgefield.

Chris Breece, senior content manager for Golfbet, recommends taking Ryan Gerard over Jackson Koivun (+110) on FanDuel, noting Gerard’s top‑10 streak, and he spots Tom Kim (+330) as a solid tie‑eligible pick given his affinity for the venue.

The experts also remind players that PGA TOUR Fantasy Golf lineups consist of four starters—including a captain—and two bench spots that can be rotated after each round, with each golfer limited to three uses per season segment.

## Why it matters
With the FedExCup Playoffs bubble forming, a strong finish at Wyndham can vault a player into the top 70 and secure a playoff berth, while fantasy managers who capture the right low‑odds players can earn a captain’s bonus and climb the fantasy rankings. Prop bettors benefit from the inflated odds on emerging names, turning modest stakes into sizable returns if the underdogs perform.

## How to think about it
Start by slotting the recommended captain and starters—Brown, Echavarria, Gerard, and Kim—into your fantasy roster, then use the bench spots for players with strong weekend rounds. Monitor live odds and adjust prop bets if an underdog shows early momentum, but keep a portion of your stake on more stable favorites to hedge volatility.

<Callout type="warning">Do not allocate the majority of your fantasy budget to underdogs; a missed cut can cripple your lineup.</Callout>
