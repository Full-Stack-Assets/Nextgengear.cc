Tucson is bracing for a classic summer showdown. Temperatures will climb to near 103°F with heat‑index values topping 107°F. Winds are expected to gust up to 20 mph before calming. Afternoon thunderstorms carry a 40 percent chance of rain, and the heat will linger into the weekend. Residents should prepare for both scorching heat and sudden downpours.

## What happened
The morning will start sunny, shifting to partly sunny by afternoon, with a high near 103°F and a heat index up to 107°F. Light northwest winds of 5‑10 mph will increase to gusts of 20 mph.
A 40 percent chance of showers and thunderstorms is forecast before 11 pm, bringing potential flooding as heavy rainfall moves in. Overnight lows will drop to the high 70s, with winds calming.
## Why it matters
Heat index above 105°F raises risk of heat‑related illness, especially for outdoor workers and seniors. Thunderstorm gusts can produce sudden downpours that overwhelm drainage, leading to flash flooding on low‑lying streets.

## How to think about it
Limit strenuous activity to early morning hours and stay hydrated with electrolyte‑rich fluids. Keep windows and blinds closed during peak heat, and use fans or air‑conditioning if possible. Monitor local alerts for thunderstorm warnings and avoid low‑lying areas that could flood. Plan indoor errands for the late afternoon when storms are most likely.
<Callout type="warning">Do not ignore heat‑index warnings; prolonged exposure can lead to heat exhaustion or heat stroke.</Callout>
