The Maryland Lottery announced that a ticket bought at a 7‑Eleven in College Park hit five of the six Powerball numbers, earning a $1 million prize. The ticket matched the numbers 6, 17, 27, 48 and 50 but missed the Powerball 5, leaving the $748 million jackpot untouched. This marks the second straight drawing in the state to produce a $1 million winner. With more than 30,000 Maryland players collecting prizes, the draw underscores the lottery’s continued popularity. The swelling jackpot now tops $748 million, setting the stage for a potentially record‑breaking Monday drawing.

<Callout type="takeaway">A five‑number match netted a $1 million win while the $748 million jackpot remains up for grabs.</Callout>

## What happened
A Powerball ticket sold at the 7‑Eleven located at 8905 Rhode Island Ave. in College Park matched the first five numbers—6, 17, 27, 48 and 50—but missed the Powerball number 5, resulting in a $1 million prize. The same drawing also produced a $50,000 prize for a ticket that matched four of the first five numbers plus the Powerball, sold at Max Liquors in Catonsville.

In total, 30,658 Maryland Lottery players won prizes ranging from $4 to the jackpot, and they have 182 days to claim their winnings. Because no ticket matched all six numbers, the jackpot grew to an estimated $748 million annuity, with a cash option of $325.1 million, for the next Monday drawing.

## Why it matters
The back‑to‑back $1 million wins highlight how even partial matches can generate life‑changing payouts, keeping player interest high despite the astronomical odds of hitting the full jackpot. A larger jackpot fuels ticket sales statewide, boosting lottery revenue that funds public programs. However, the continued absence of a jackpot winner also underscores the slim probability of a full match, reminding players to gamble responsibly.

## How to think about it
Treat lottery tickets as entertainment rather than an investment strategy. Set a strict budget—only spend what you can afford to lose—and consider each ticket a small gamble with a tiny chance of a large payout. If you choose to play, focus on the excitement of the draw rather than the expectation of winning, and remember that the odds of matching all six numbers are roughly 1 in 292 million.

<Callout type="warning">Playing the lottery carries a high risk of loss; never spend more than a modest, discretionary amount.</Callout>
