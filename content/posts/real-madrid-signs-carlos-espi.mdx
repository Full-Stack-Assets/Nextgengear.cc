Real Madrid announced the signing of 21‑year‑old striker Carlos Espí from Levante. The forward joins the club on a five‑year contract that runs through June 2031. Espí arrives after scoring 20 goals in 66 appearances for Levante and being named the league’s best U‑23 player last season. The addition bolsters Mourinho’s attacking options as the Spanish giants look to refresh their forward line this summer.

<Callout type="takeaway">Espí provides Real Madrid with a proven La Liga scorer and long‑term attacking depth.</Callout>

## What happened
The club and Levante reached an agreement on Thursday, activating Espí’s €25 million release clause and securing a five‑year deal that keeps him at the Bernabéu until 2031.

During his three seasons with Levante, the 21‑year‑old appeared in 66 matches, netting 20 goals, including 11 goals in 25 La Liga games last campaign, and earned the best U‑23 player award in the Spanish league.

Espí’s move follows a busy transfer window for Madrid, which also saw the arrivals of Bernardo Silva, Marc Cucurella, Ibrahima Konaté and Denzel Dumfries, as well as the appointment of José Mourinho as head coach.

## Why it matters
Adding a forward with a strong aerial presence (6 ft 4 in) gives Mourinho a tactical option that has been missing from the squad. Espí’s proven goal‑scoring record at a young age reduces the financial risk compared with higher‑priced signings, while his experience in Spain’s top two divisions should ease his transition. However, he will compete with established attackers for minutes, and his ability to perform under the pressure of a club of Real’s stature remains to be proven.
