The hunt for Daniel Craig’s successor is heating up as Action Network releases its latest odds. Callum Turner has surged back to the top with +146 odds, translating to an implied 40.68% probability of landing the role. Auditions continue this month, and Amazon aims to lock in a new 007 before year‑end for a 2027 production start. This shift reshapes fan expectations and studio strategy alike.

## What happened
Action Network’s updated tracker shows Callum Turner reclaiming the lead with +146 odds, equating to a 40.68% implied probability. Behind him, Tom Francis sits at +333 odds and Louis Partridge at +455, while Harris Dickinson and Aaron Taylor‑Johnson round out the top five.

Auditions are ongoing, with the next round slated for later this month. Amazon, which recently acquired the franchise rights, intends to finalize a new 007 before the end of the year and begin filming the next installment in 2027.

The odds reflect a dynamic shift since September 2025, when Turner first rose to the top before briefly yielding to Anthony Boyle and Jacob Elordi. The current list also includes names like Jack Lowden, Jacob Elordi, and Dónal Finn further down the rankings.
