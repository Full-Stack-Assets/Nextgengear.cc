The Toronto Blue Jays have completed a trade that sends outfielder Daulton Varsho to the Houston Astros. In return, Houston parts with left‑handed reliever Spencer Arrighetti, who has been pitching in the majors this season. The move addresses roster needs for both clubs as the Jays look to bolster their bullpen and the Astros add depth to their outfield. With the trade deadline looming, the transaction could influence playoff positioning for both teams.

<Callout type="takeaway">The Blue Jays acquire a controllable left‑handed arm while the Astros add a versatile outfielder.</Callout>

## What happened
Daulton Varsho, a .243 hitter with a 92 wRC+ rating, is headed to Houston. The outfielder has struggled defensively, often described as limited in both hitting and throwing, but he brings a left‑handed bat and occasional power bursts that the Astros hope to tap.

Spencer Arrighetti, a left‑handed reliever, joins Toronto. He ranks in the top 6% for hard‑hit rate and posts a 13% walk rate, placing him among the top 11% in that metric. Arrighetti has three years of team control and, despite a recent injury that cut short a start, offers upside as a middle‑relief option.

## Why it matters
For the Blue Jays, gaining a pitcher with strong peripheral numbers adds depth to a bullpen that has been a late‑season weakness. The Astros, meanwhile, acquire an everyday outfielder who can fill a roster spot and provide a left‑handed bat in a lineup that lacks depth at the bottom. Both clubs are positioning themselves for the final stretch of the season, where roster flexibility can be a decisive factor.
