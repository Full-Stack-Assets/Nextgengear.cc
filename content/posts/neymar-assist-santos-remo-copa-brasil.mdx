Santos secured a 1-0 victory over Remo at the Mangueirão on Tuesday, advancing to the Copa do Brasil quarter‑finals. The decisive goal came from Rony, but it was Neymar’s incisive pass after coming off the bench that unlocked the defense. The win not only moves the club into the next round but also adds a R$4 million prize from the CBF, raising the stakes for the upcoming league matches.

<Callout type="takeaway">Neymar’s late‑game impact turned a tight match into a decisive win for Santos.</Callout>

## What happened
The two‑leg tie had been deadlocked after a goalless draw in the first match, and the second leg saw an early red card for Remo defender Marllon, giving Santos a numerical advantage. Santos pressed, but both sides missed chances, and the game remained 0-0 deep into the second half.

At halftime, coach Cuca introduced Neymar, who immediately influenced the play by delivering a through ball that Rony converted in the 28th minute of the second half. Neymar also missed a clear chance later, but his involvement shifted the momentum decisively in Santos’ favor.

## Why it matters
The victory propels Santos into the Copa do Brasil quarter‑finals and secures a R$4 million payout, boosting the club’s finances ahead of the Brasileirão. It also highlights Neymar’s ability to change games even when not starting, reinforcing his value to the squad. For Remo, the loss underscores the challenge of competing against teams with star power and depth.
