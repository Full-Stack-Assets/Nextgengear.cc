## What happened
On August 5, the Cubs submitted paperwork to Major League Baseball to activate Pete Crow‑Armstrong from the Triple‑A Iowa Cubs. The roster change was made official during the second game of the series at Dodger Stadium.
Crow‑Armstrong, a left‑handed hitter who primarily plays center field, replaces an injured infielder on the 26‑man roster. The club also optioned a reliever to make room for the outfielder.
