The Ocypus Sigma L36 Pro arrives with a detachable 3.5‑inch IPS screen and a price tag that undercuts most comparable AIOs. Tom's Hardware put the cooler through maximum‑strength workloads on a Ryzen 9 9950X3D to gauge its thermal ceiling. Despite its budget price, the unit packs fans rated at 5.8 mmH₂O static pressure, promising strong cooling. The review asks whether the combination of a cheap LCD and solid performance makes this cooler a smart buy for performance‑oriented builders.

<Callout type="takeaway">The Sigma L36 Pro delivers high‑end cooling and a large LCD at a budget‑friendly $139 price. </Callout>

## What happened
Tom's Hardware tested the Sigma L36 Pro on a high‑end Ryzen 9 9950X3D, measuring temperatures under sustained load. The cooler kept the CPU within safe limits, matching or beating many pricier AIOs that lack a display. The detachable 3.5‑inch screen, with a 640×480 VGA resolution, showed real‑time metrics and could play animated images.

The review also noted that the fans can become noisy at full speed, but Ocypus includes a speed‑reduction PWM cable to tame the sound. Pricing was confirmed at $139 (or less in some markets), positioning the unit well below other LCD‑equipped AIOs.

## Why it matters
AIO coolers with integrated displays have typically commanded premium prices, limiting them to enthusiasts willing to pay extra for aesthetics. By offering a large LCD and strong thermal performance at a sub‑$150 price, Ocypus challenges the notion that visual flair must come at a cost. Builders seeking both function and style can now consider the Sigma L36 Pro without breaking the budget, potentially shifting market expectations for future coolers.

## How to think about it
If you prioritize cooling performance but also want a visual readout, the Sigma L36 Pro offers a rare blend of both at a price most builders can afford. Pair it with the included PWM cable to keep noise in check, and consider using the display mainly for system monitoring rather than media playback, given its low resolution. For budget‑conscious builds that still crave a premium look, this AIO is a compelling choice.
