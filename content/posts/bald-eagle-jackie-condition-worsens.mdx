Jackie the bald eagle, a star of the Big Bear Eagle Nest Cam, received a concerning health update from the Ojai Raptor Center. Recent bloodwork showed her packed cell volume fell to 13%, down from 17% just a week earlier. The decline follows a recent fight with two other eagles that forced her away from her Big Bear nest for over two weeks. Veterinarians have begun advanced diagnostic testing to pinpoint the cause. The situation underscores how fragile rehabilitated wildlife can be, even under expert care.

<Callout type="takeaway">Jackie's health setback highlights the ongoing challenges of rehabilitating injured raptors.</Callout>

## What happened
The Ojai Raptor Center posted on Facebook that today's bloodwork revealed Jackie’s packed cell volume dropped to 13%, a metric indicating severe anemia. The drop follows an earlier improvement that had raised hopes for a quicker recovery.

Jackie was rescued after a territorial clash with two other eagles and has been housed at the center for a little over two weeks, away from her nest and family. While she showed signs of anemia improvement last week, the new results prompted the team to request expedited laboratory testing and additional advanced diagnostics.

Millions of viewers follow Jackie and her mate Shadow through the nonprofit’s livestream cameras, which have documented the pair’s breeding successes, including eaglets Sandy and Luna born in April.

## Why it matters
Jackie's decline spotlights the delicate balance of wildlife rehabilitation, where even minor setbacks can have serious consequences. Public attention generated by the nest cam translates into financial support and volunteer interest for the Ojai Raptor Center, directly affecting its capacity to treat other injured raptors. Moreover, the case provides valuable clinical data that can improve veterinary protocols for bald eagles nationwide.
