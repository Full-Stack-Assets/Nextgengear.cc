Comic‑Con 2026’s third day turned the spotlight on animation veteran Brad Bird, who debuted a sneak peek of his new film Ray Gunn. The panel also delivered the first concrete update on Incredibles 3, confirming Bird’s co‑writing role and Peter Sohn’s directorial debut. A surprise anecdote about Tom Cruise’s early stunt on Mission: Impossible added a playful behind‑the‑scenes flavor. These announcements signal a shift toward hybrid storytelling that blends classic animation sensibilities with live‑action ambition, setting the tone for the rest of the convention.

## What happened
Brad Bird used his Comic‑Con panel to showcase the first seven minutes of Ray Gunn, an animated feature he describes as “Buck Rogers meets The Maltese Falcon.” The teaser follows Ray (voiced by Sam Rockwell) as he investigates an alien‑smuggling plot, leading to a chase that highlights the film’s retro‑fantasy aesthetic and hints at the blend of animation with live‑action sequences he is developing.

The director also addressed the long‑awaited Incredibles 3, confirming he is co‑writing and co‑producing while longtime Pixar artist Peter Sohn will direct. Although plot details were withheld, composer Michael Giacchino appeared onstage, underscoring the film’s creative continuity. In a lighter moment, Bird recounted Tom Cruise’s first stunt attempt for Mission: Impossible — Ghost Protocol — noting the humor it generated for the audience.

## Why it matters
Ray Gunn’s early reveal suggests Pixar‑style talent is exploring original IPs beyond the studio’s traditional slate, potentially expanding the market for adult‑oriented animation. The Incredibles 3 update reassures fans that the franchise remains active, with a fresh director who may inject new visual storytelling while preserving the series’ core humor. Together, these announcements illustrate a broader industry trend of hybridizing animation and live‑action expertise, which could influence future project greenlights and audience expectations.
