## What happened
The Powerball drawing on July 29 produced the main numbers 30‑36‑40‑42‑57, a Powerball of 02, and a Power Play multiplier of 2, as reported by the Greenville Online article. The South Carolina Education Lottery also published the Pick 3 Plus Fireball results for the same draw, though the specific three‑digit combination was not detailed in the source.

In addition to the winning numbers, the lottery office reiterated its tiered claim process: prizes up to $500 can be redeemed at any authorized retailer, amounts between $501 and $100,000 require mailing the ticket and claim form, and winnings above $100,000 must be claimed in person at the headquarters in Columbia. All claims must be filed within 180 days of the draw date.

## How to think about it
First, verify that your ticket matches the published numbers before initiating any claim. If your prize is $500 or less, head to the nearest authorized retailer for an immediate payout. For mid‑range winnings ($501‑$100,000), complete the claim form, attach a copy of a government‑issued ID, and mail the package using registered mail to protect your ticket. For larger prizes, plan a visit to the South Carolina Lottery Headquarters in Columbia and bring your signed ticket, claim form, ID, and Social Security card; consider setting up an Electronic Funds Transfer for faster receipt. Finally, treat lottery play as entertainment—budget only what you can afford to lose and be aware of the odds.
